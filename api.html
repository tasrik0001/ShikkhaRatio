Data downloads / JSON files

The numbers, ready for your own project.

Every figure on ShikkhaRatio is published twice: on these pages and as simple JSON files. Open a link in your browser, or ask for it from your own site. Credit BANBEIS 2024 when you share the figures.

These are plain data files any website can read.

The files live right here on this site, so any page may fetch them. No sign-up, no keys, no special setup.

The links

School and college figures for 2024, district by district. Press Copy to grab a link.
MethodWhat you getAddressCopy
GET Site version, build date, national ratios and row counts /api/v1/meta.json
GET School rows for all 64 districts /api/v1/schools.json
GET College rows for all 64 districts /api/v1/colleges.json
GET One district: school and college rows, ranks and the national comparison /api/v1/districts/{slug}.json
GET Minimum, maximum, median and mean ratios by sector /api/v1/stats.json
GET Published top 5 lists plus the 10 highest and 10 lowest districts /api/v1/top-pressure.json
GET The eight divisions with average district ratios /api/v1/divisions.json

Each district has its own file. The name is the district in lower case, with apostrophes removed and spaces turned into hyphens: /api/v1/districts/dhaka.json and /api/v1/districts/coxs-bazar.json. Every row carries a Slug value you can use to build the path.

What the numbers mean

School and college rows use the same field names as the CSV downloads, so the two formats line up.

Division
One of the eight administrative divisions.
District
One of the 64 districts.
Slug
The lower case file name for this district.
Inst_Total
Institutions in the district.
Inst_Girls
Girls' institutions.
Tchr_Total
Teachers.
Tchr_Female
Female teachers.
Tchr_%Female
Female teachers as a percentage.
Stud_Total
Students.
Stud_Girls
Girl students.
Stud_%Girls
Girl students as a percentage.
TSR
Students per teacher, the ratio shown across this site.

Map file and spreadsheets

The district map file is large, so it is not copied into the API. It stays at data/bgd-admin2.geojson. Prefer spreadsheets? The CSV downloads are listed on the Sources page, and you can read about coverage on Limitations.
