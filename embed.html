No account needed

The snippet loads a live frame from this site, so the figures stay up to date whenever the data page updates. You do not need to copy any numbers by hand.

Paste the line into the HTML of your page. Most site builders and blogs accept a raw HTML block. The frame is 800 pixels wide and 600 pixels tall, with a strong border that matches the look of this site.

Please credit BANBEIS 2024 and ShikkhaRatio under the frame so readers know where the figures come from. The district profile uses ?d=slug, the explorer can open with ?district=Name, and the charts page accepts the same district slug.
