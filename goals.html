Our purpose

Make education data part of the conversation.

I want more people to notice how students and teachers are distributed across Bangladesh. A clear number, connected to a familiar place, can be the beginning of a useful question.

Make the figures easier to understand

You should not need to know your way around a spreadsheet to find your district’s figures. ShikkhaRatio brings students, teachers and institutions together and explains the ratio in ordinary language.

The goal is awareness, not the most dramatic headline. I want readers to understand what a number counts and why the people behind it matter. The articles and full data downloads offer a way in alongside the explorer.

Encourage better questions about staffing

Why do some districts have more students per teacher than others? How do those differences look inside individual schools? Are the teachers available able to cover the subjects students need?

I hope the project can eventually help educators, government and organisations working on education think about teacher distribution. For now, its contribution is to make district comparisons easier to explore and discuss.

Build on the work already done

The district summaries have been corrected and checked against the workbook’s district, division and national totals. The record of those corrections explains what changed. Better evidence means keeping that work visible as the project grows.

The next step is to compare the workbook with the original publication and clarify its coverage. Beyond that, institution-level counts, subject needs and staffing records would help connect a broad district picture to more specific questions.

Keep the human reason in view

This began with memories of my mother’s workload, not with a map. Teachers’ and students’ accounts would add something the district totals cannot: a sense of everyday life in the places we are comparing.

I would like this project to help people ask better questions and find the evidence to answer them. That is a small contribution toward a much larger goal: an education system where teachers have the time and support to teach.

Now and next

Start with understanding

Now: explore 64 districts, compare the two sectors separately and download the figures.

Next: check the original publication and document coverage more fully.

Longer term: bring in institution-level staffing information and accounts from teachers and students.
