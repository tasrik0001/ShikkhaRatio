Sources / Dataset year 2024

Follow the figures back to their source.

The explorer uses district summaries made from a workbook based on BANBEIS 2024 education data. Here you can find the publication reference, understand the calculation and download the records.

Publication and data

Publication: Bangladesh Education Statistics 2024, Bangladesh Bureau of Educational Information and Statistics (BANBEIS).

Project data: a BANBEIS-based workbook covering secondary schools and colleges, summarised for all 64 districts. ShikkhaRatio calculates the ratios from these records. The corrected calculations have been checked against the workbook’s district, division and national totals.

How the ratio works

Students per teacher = total students ÷ total teachers. The explorer calls this TSR and displays it to two decimal places. The national ratio divides the national student count by the national teacher count, rather than averaging district ratios.

The World Bank’s secondary pupil-teacher ratio metadata provides a reference for this definition. A pupil-teacher ratio is not the size of an actual class.

Take the data with you

  • Full secondary-school summary (.csv): all 64 districts and the national total.
  • Full college summary (.csv): all 64 districts and the national total.
  • Source workbook (.xlsx): the working input for both summaries.
  • Source register (.csv): references, calculation methods, correction history and remaining checks.
  • Choose a custom CSV export: select the sector and district in the explorer.

Credit the source

When sharing these figures, credit BANBEIS 2024 as the underlying data source and ShikkhaRatio for the workbook-based district summaries and calculations. Include the sector, year and a link to this page so readers can find the same records.

For coverage, source checks and the correction history, read Limitations.
